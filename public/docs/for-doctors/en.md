# DocPats for Physicians

DocPats is the physician's workplace: patient consultations, patient charts, AI assistance in reviewing clinical material, and professional training in a single account. No clinic is required: you work for yourself, and if you eventually open a clinic, it can be activated within the same account, with no data migration.

**The first 3 months after registration are free, on the limits of the Start plan:**
100 patients in your practice, 15 case reviews and 15 discharge summaries per month, 240 minutes of video.
This is not a stripped-down demo, but the very same plan that costs $19 in the lineup.
No payment details are required for the trial period.

When the three months end, the account switches to **Lite — $9 per month**, the least expensive plan, unless you choose a different one yourself. No one is enrolled in anything above the entry tier: the choice of plan remains yours.

The Lite limits are more modest: up to 30 patients, 5 case reviews and 5 discharge summaries per month, 60 minutes of video. **Patients already entered will not disappear** — the restriction applies only to adding new ones. If your practice has outgrown Lite, choose Start, Growth, or Pro.

**The platform does not take a percentage of your consultations.** Whatever the patient paid is what you receive: settlement occurs directly between you and the patient; DocPats takes no part in it and earns only from the subscription.

## Consultations and payment

Patients search for a physician on the platform and book appointments themselves. You configure your schedule: open slots by day and appointment type, closed days, with booking confirmation up to you.

- **Video consultation** — directly in the browser, with no software installation. The volume of included minutes depends on the plan, from 60 to 1200 per month; exact figures are in the "What it costs" section.
- **Payment for the consultation goes to you in full** — the platform withholds no percentage.
- Chat with the patient between consultations: messaging, files, history.

## A patient chart without a clinic

You have your own outpatient practice inside your account, and it contains two types of patients: those **registered** on the platform (they see their own data and grant you access) and **private** patients — those you manage yourself, without an account of their own.

The chart is not free text, but a structure familiar to physicians:

- complaints, anamnesis morbi, anamnesis vitae;
- status praesens and status localis;
- laboratory test results;
- CT, MRI, and ultrasound reports;
- recommendations;
- allergies, chronic conditions, immunizations, family history.

Repeat examinations are assembled from templates rather than typed anew. How many patients fit in your practice depends on the plan — see "What it costs."

## AI that does not make the diagnosis for you

The assistant reviews clinical material: laboratory tests, ECG, CT, MRI, ultrasound, and radiography reports, endoscopy, histology, dermatoscopy — and the clinical case as a whole. It indicates what to pay attention to, what to clarify, and **what is missing from the data**.

The boundary here is maintained not by wording in the interface, but by the very architecture of the system:

- every output is labeled as supportive, and this label is immutable — it travels with the data into any export or integration;
- a case cannot be closed until you have written your own conclusion;
- the model's output is never inserted into your conclusion automatically.

You write and sign the final conclusion. The system remains supportive — and this is a deliberate decision, not a fine-print disclaimer.

Separately: a **discharge summary in SOAP format** is assembled from the visit chart, leaving you only to proofread it. Monthly quotas for case reviews and discharge summaries follow the plan — see "What it costs."

## Exam preparation

A bank of **1011 questions** — board certification, residency, international certifications, continuing medical education. Questions are sorted by category and program, attempts are saved, and errors are collected into a separate queue for review — you return precisely to what gave you trouble.

There are four modes, divided into study and examination formats:

- **With explanations** — an explanation immediately after each answer, no timer.
- **Weak-topic reinforcement** — the question set is assembled from your statistics on previous attempts.
- **Timed** — the timer runs, explanations only at the end.
- **Full simulation** — question set according to the official exam blueprint, timer, final report.

The first two are available to everyone and are unlimited in the number of questions. The two examination modes are included in the Growth and Pro plans, and can be purchased as an add-on on lower plans — see "What it costs."

## Your reputation and visibility

- **Verification**: two levels of confirmation — basic and full, based on documents.
- **Articles** — both general and scientific, under your authorship.
- **Patient reviews** and **recommendations from physician colleagues**.
- Your profile and articles are **visible to search engines**: the page is served with ready-made titles, descriptions, and schema.org markup, rather than as an empty shell that a search engine cannot read. Patients find you through search, not only within the platform.

## Language is no obstacle to work

The interface is available in five languages: Russian, English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, and Arabic, with correct text direction for Arabic.

**Chat messages are translated** — you write in your language, the patient reads in theirs. The platform's educational materials are also translated automatically, rather than waiting for an editor.

## Patient data

- Personal and medical data are **encrypted** at rest.
- Every access to a patient chart is written to an **audit log** that can be neither modified nor deleted, and that is retained for seven years.
- A registered patient **personally grants** your access to their data.

The platform is built with regard to HIPAA requirements for handling medical data.

## If you grow into a clinic

The clinic is activated within the same account: staff with nine roles and permissions down to the level of individual sections, departments, rooms, equipment, scheduling and queue management, invoices and payments, pharmacy and inventory, case conferences, telemedicine, a knowledge base, staff announcements, and analytics.

Plus **your own clinic website** — assembled with a builder inside the platform, with a public storefront, reviews, services, and request intake.

## Working, but still being populated

Two sections are already built and open; they simply do not yet contain much content:

- **Imaging interpretation simulator** — the physician does not select an answer option, but works as a radiologist: locating the pathology directly on the image, following an interpretation protocol, making a diagnosis, and receiving an analysis. Five interpretation modalities — radiography, CT, MRI, ultrasound, ECG; three stations — images, laboratory tests, virtual patient; duels with colleagues and a leaderboard. The first cases are being published now.
- **Anthropometry in plastic surgery** — marking of anatomical landmarks on photographs, automatic calculation of angles and proportions, before-and-after comparison. The first procedure is rhinoplasty.

## What it costs

**The platform earns only from subscriptions.** There is no percentage taken from consultations on any plan: whatever the patient paid is what you receive.

**Trial period, 3 months — free.** For all new physicians, on the limits of the Start plan. At its conclusion, the account switches to Lite — the least expensive plan — unless you choose a different one. No payment details are required for the trial period, and no charge will occur without your involvement.

- **Lite — $9 per month** ($90 per year). A simple entry point: up to 30 patients in your practice, 5 case reviews and 5 discharge summaries per month, 60 minutes of video.
- **Start — $19 per month** ($190 per year). A small practice: up to 100 patients, 15 case reviews and 15 discharge summaries, 240 minutes of video.
- **Growth — $49 per month** ($490 per year). A steady patient flow: up to 600 patients, 40 case reviews and 40 discharge summaries, 600 minutes of video.
- **Pro — $99 per month** ($990 per year). A large practice: up to 2000 patients, 100 case reviews and 100 discharge summaries, 1200 minutes of video.

Case reviews and discharge summaries are counted monthly, and this is not a formality: each of them is a call to a language model for which the platform pays real money. The figures in the plans have been chosen with a wide margin relative to ordinary work; they exist so that a malfunction or an accidental loop does not cost thousands of dollars, not to restrict your work.

**For clinics** — a separate lineup:

- **Clinic Start — $99 per month** ($990 per year). Up to 5 physicians.
- **Clinic Business — $249 per month** ($2490 per year). Up to 15 physicians, analytics and priority in recommendations.
- **Clinic Enterprise — $499 per month** ($4990 per year). Up to 50 physicians. More — under a separate agreement.

**Exam preparation** is purchased separately and layers on top of any plan. You can study for free: the modes with explanations and weak-topic reinforcement are open to everyone and do not count questions. What is paid for is the rehearsal of the exam itself:

- **Exam Prep Plus — $7 per month** ($70 per year). Adds timed testing.
- **Exam Prep Unlimited — $15 per month** ($150 per year). Adds full simulation: question set according to the exam blueprint, timer, final report.

On Growth and Pro, both modes are already included — there is nothing to purchase. The add-on is needed on the trial period, Lite, and Start, and it is also intended for students and residents who need only the tests and not the rest of the platform.

Annual payment equals ten monthly payments: two months free. Current terms are always on the [pricing page](/pricing).

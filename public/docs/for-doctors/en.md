# DocPats for Physicians

DocPats is a physician's workplace: patient appointments, their charts, AI assistance in analyzing clinical material, and professional training in a single account. You do not need a clinic for this: you work for yourself, and if you do open a clinic, it is enabled within the same account, with no data migration.

**The first 6 months after registration are free, at Growth plan limits:** 600 patients in your practice, 60 case analyses and 60 clinical summaries per month, 720 minutes of video. This is not a stripped-down demo, but the very plan that is priced at $49 in the lineup. No payment details are required for the trial period.

When the six months end, the account moves to **Lite — $3 per month**, the least expensive plan, unless you choose another one yourself. No one is enrolled in anything above the entry tier: the choice of plan remains yours.

The Lite limits are modest: up to 17 patients, 3 case analyses and 3 clinical summaries per month, 40 minutes of video. **Patients already entered will not go anywhere** — the restriction applies only to adding new ones. If your practice has outgrown Lite, choose Start, Growth, or Pro; the commission will also decrease: during the trial period and on Lite it is the same, 15%, and beyond that it only goes down.

## Appointments and Payment

Patients search for a physician on the platform and book appointments themselves. You configure your schedule: open slots by day and appointment type, closed days, with booking confirmation up to you.

- **Video consultations** — directly in the browser, with no software installation. The volume of included minutes depends on the plan, from 40 to 1500 per month; exact figures are in the "What It Costs" section.
- **The platform commission** depends on the plan: 15% during the trial period and on Lite, 13% on Start, 12% on Growth, 10% on Pro. The rest is yours — the full list is below, in the "What It Costs" section.
- Chat with the patient between appointments: messages, files, history.

## The Patient Chart Without a Clinic

You have your own outpatient practice inside your account, and it contains two types of patients: those **registered** on the platform (they see their own data and confirm your access) and **private** ones — those you manage yourself, without an account of their own.

The chart is not free text, but a structure familiar to physicians:

- complaints, anamnesis morbi, anamnesis vitae;
- status praesens and status localis;
- laboratory test results;
- CT, MRI, and ultrasound reports;
- recommendations;
- allergies, chronic conditions, immunizations, family history.

Repeat examinations are assembled from templates rather than typed anew. How many patients fit in your practice depends on the plan — see "What It Costs."

## AI That Does Not Diagnose in Your Place

The assistant analyzes clinical material: laboratory tests, ECG, CT, MRI, ultrasound and radiography reports, endoscopy, histology, dermoscopy — and the clinical case as a whole. It shows what to pay attention to, what to clarify, and **what is missing from the data**.

The boundary here is maintained not by wording in the interface, but by the very design of the system:

- every output is flagged as supportive, and this flag is immutable — it travels with the data into any export and integration;
- a case cannot be closed until you write your own conclusion;
- the model's output is never inserted into your conclusion automatically.

You write and sign the final conclusion. The system remains supportive — and this is a deliberate decision, not a disclaimer in fine print.

Separately: **a clinical summary in SOAP format** is assembled from the visit chart, leaving you only to proofread it. Monthly quotas for case analyses and summaries follow your plan — see "What It Costs."

## Exam Preparation

A bank of **1011 questions** — certification, residency, international certificates, continuing medical education. Questions are sorted by category and program, attempts are saved, and errors are collected into a separate queue for review — you return precisely to what you did not master.

There are four modes, divided into study and exam modes:

- **With explanations** — an explanation immediately after each answer, no timer.
- **Weak-topic drilling** — the question set is assembled from your statistics on previous attempts.
- **Timed** — the timer runs, explanations only at the end.
- **Full simulation** — the question set follows the official exam blueprint, with a timer and a final report.

The first two are available to everyone and are not limited by number of questions. The two exam modes are included in the Growth and Pro plans, and can be purchased separately on the lower plans — see "What It Costs."

## Your Reputation and Visibility

- **Verification**: two levels of confirmation — basic and full, based on documents.
- **Articles** — general and scientific, published under your authorship.
- **Patient reviews** and **recommendations from fellow physicians**.
- Your profile and articles are **visible to search engines**: the page is served with ready-made titles, description, and schema.org markup, rather than as an empty shell that a search engine cannot read. Patients find you through search, not only inside the platform.

## Language Does Not Get in the Way

The interface is available in five languages: Russian, English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, and Arabic, with correct text direction for Arabic.

**Chat messages are translated** — you write in your language, the patient reads in theirs. The platform's educational materials are also translated automatically, rather than waiting for an editor.

## Patient Data

- Personal and medical data are **encrypted** at rest.
- Every access to a patient chart is written to an **audit log** that can neither be modified nor deleted, and that is retained for seven years.
- A registered patient **personally confirms** your access to their data.

The platform is built with HIPAA requirements for handling medical data in mind.

## If You Grow Into a Clinic

A clinic is enabled within the same account: staff members with nine roles and permissions down to the level of an individual section, departments, rooms, equipment, scheduling and queue, invoices and payments, pharmacy and inventory, case conferences, telemedicine, a knowledge base, staff announcements, analytics.

Plus **your own clinic website** — built with a site builder inside the platform, with a public showcase, reviews, services, and intake of requests.

## Working, but Still Being Filled Out

Two sections are already built and open; they simply do not have much content yet:

- **Image reading trainer** — the physician does not choose an answer option, but works as a radiologist: locating the pathology directly on the image, following the examination protocol, making a diagnosis, and receiving an analysis. Five reading modalities — radiography, CT, MRI, ultrasound, ECG; three stations — images, laboratory tests, virtual patient; duels with colleagues and a ranking. The first cases are being published now.
- **Anthropometry in plastic surgery** — marking anatomical landmarks on photographs, automatic calculation of angles and proportions, before-and-after comparison. The first procedure is rhinoplasty.

## What It Costs

**Trial period, 6 months — free.** For all new physicians, at Growth plan limits, with a 15% commission. When it ends, the account moves to Lite — the least expensive plan — unless you choose another one. No payment details are required for the trial period, and no charge will occur without your involvement.

- **Lite — $3 per month** ($30 per year). The simplest entry point: up to 17 patients in your practice, 3 case analyses and 3 clinical summaries per month, 40 minutes of video, 15% commission.
- **Start — $19 per month** ($190 per year). A small practice: up to 100 patients, 20 case analyses and 20 clinical summaries, 240 minutes of video, 13% commission.
- **Growth — $49 per month** ($490 per year). A steady flow of patients: up to 600 patients, 60 case analyses and 60 clinical summaries, 720 minutes of video, 12% commission.
- **Pro — $99 per month** ($990 per year). No limits on patients, case analyses, or clinical summaries, 1500 minutes of video, 10% commission.

The commission works inversely to the subscription: the cheaper the plan, the larger the share the platform takes from each appointment. The trial period is equivalent to the entry tier, so moving to any paid plan does not increase your commission.

**For clinics** there is a separate lineup, and its commission decreases as the plan grows:

- **Clinic Start — $99 per month** ($990 per year). Up to 5 physicians, 10% commission.
- **Clinic Business — $249 per month** ($2490 per year). Up to 15 physicians, 7% commission, analytics and priority in recommendations.
- **Clinic Enterprise — $499 per month** ($4990 per year). No limit on the number of physicians, 5% commission.

**Exam preparation** is purchased separately and layers on top of any plan. You can study for free: the modes with explanations and weak-topic drilling are open to everyone and do not count questions. What is paid for is the rehearsal of the exam itself:

- **Exam Prep Plus — $7 per month** ($70 per year). Adds timed sessions.
- **Exam Prep Unlimited — $15 per month** ($150 per year). Adds full simulation: the question set per the exam blueprint, a timer, and a final report.

During the trial period and on Growth and Pro, both modes are already included — there is nothing to buy. The add-on is needed on Lite and Start, and it is also intended for students and residents who need only the tests and not the rest of the platform.

Annual payment equals ten monthly payments: two months free. Current terms are always on the [pricing page](/pricing).

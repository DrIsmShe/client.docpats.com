# DocPats for Patients

Your medical history is kept by you, not in someone else's records room: visits,
laboratory results, imaging, immunizations, and past surgeries are gathered in
one place. You decide which physicians see what, and you can retrieve your data
at any time.

**You can start without registering** — ask a question and get an answer before
creating an account.

---

## Ask before the visit

The medical assistant reviews your complaints, explains what your laboratory
results mean, and suggests which specialist to see. It answers in your language —
the one in which you addressed it.

What it does **differently from a search engine**:

- **it does not make a diagnosis** — it provides a preliminary clinical
  assessment and always recommends an in-person visit;
- with red-flag symptoms — chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke —
  it immediately advises calling emergency services rather than continuing the
  conversation;
- it responds only to medical topics.

After several messages it will offer to compile a **clinical summary** — a brief
digest of the conversation: complaints, their duration, what has already been
taken, what should be clarified. You can bring it to your physician and not have
to recall in the office exactly when everything began.

This is not a substitute for a physician. It is preparation for the visit, so
that the appointment starts with what matters.

## Your medical history — in one place

- **Visits** — past and upcoming, with history.
- **Laboratory results** and **examinations** — findings entered by your
  physician.
- **Imaging reports**, past surgeries, immunizations, allergies, chronic
  conditions.
- **My Physicians** and **My Clinics** — everyone you have consulted.

Your data is not tied to a single clinic: if you change physicians or cities,
the history stays with you. **You can export your data without restrictions on
any plan, including the free one.**

## You decide who sees what

When a physician or clinic requests access to your chart, you do not answer
"yes" or "no" for the whole record. Sections are opened **individually**:

- allergies;
- visits;
- encounters and their notes;
- imaging and reports;
- immunizations;
- past surgeries.

You may open allergies to your dentist and leave your surgical history closed.
Access is granted by you, not by an administrator at the front desk, and every
access to your chart is written to a log that can be neither altered nor
deleted.

## Your physician — in person or by video

You find a physician by specialty, review the profile, articles, reviews from
other patients, and recommendations from fellow physicians, and book an
available time slot yourself.

- **Video visits** take place in the browser — nothing needs to be installed.
- **Chat with your physician** between visits: questions, files, and the entire
  correspondence is saved.
- **Correspondence is translated**: you write in your language, your physician
  reads in theirs. The language barrier is no longer a reason to look for
  a "physician who speaks my language."

## Your data

- Personal and medical data are **encrypted** at rest.
- Every access to your chart is written to an **audit log**, which is retained
  for seven years and cannot be altered, even by an administrator.
- A physician gains access **only after your confirmation** and only to the
  sections you have opened.

The platform is built with HIPAA requirements for handling medical data in mind.

## What it costs

**Everything related to your data is free and will remain free**: your entire
history, all documents, export, booking a physician, and messaging with them.
Paying makes sense only for AI assistance and for family member profiles.

- **Free — $0.** No time limit. Your entire history, all documents, unlimited
  export, booking a physician, and chat with them. Plus 2 assistant
  consultations per month.
- **Care — $15 per year** (or $1.50 per month). Everything in the free plan plus
  **profiles for 2 family members**, medication reminders, and document
  translation into five languages. The assistant is the same as on the free
  plan: this plan is about caring for relatives, not about AI volume.
- **Plus — $9 per month** ($90 per year). 10 assistant consultations,
  **profiles for 5 family members**, reminders, and translation.
- **Pro — $19 per month** ($190 per year). 25 consultations,
  **profiles for 10 family members**, and automatic backup of your history.

Annual payment equals ten monthly payments: two months free. With Care it is
more advantageous to take the full year at once — $15 instead of $18.

Separately — **preparation for medical examinations**: if you are a student or
resident, the platform's question bank is unlocked as an add-on on top of any
plan, including the free one — **$7 per month** adds timed practice,
**$15 per month** adds full examination simulation. A patient does not need it:
the bank is compiled from physician certification questions.

Current terms are always on the [pricing page](/pricing).

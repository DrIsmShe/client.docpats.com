# DocPats for Patients

Your medical history is kept by you, not in someone else's front office: visits,
laboratory tests, imaging, immunizations, and past surgeries are all collected in
one place. You decide which physicians see what, and you can retrieve your data
at any time.

**You can start without registering** — ask a question and get an answer before
creating an account.

---

## Ask before the visit

The medical assistant reviews your complaints, explains what your test results
mean, and suggests which specialist to see. It responds in your language — the
one you used to address it.

What it does **differently from a search engine**:

- **it does not make a diagnosis** — it provides a preliminary clinical
  assessment and always recommends an in-person visit;
- with red-flag signs — chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke — it
  immediately advises calling emergency services rather than continuing the
  conversation;
- it responds only to medical topics.

After a few messages, it will offer to compile a **clinical summary** — a brief
digest of the conversation: complaints, their duration, what has already been
taken, and what should be clarified. You can bring it to your physician and
avoid having to recall in the office exactly when everything began.

This is not a substitute for a physician. It is preparation for the visit, so
that the appointment starts with what matters.

## Your medical history — in one place

- **Visits** — past and upcoming, with full history.
- **Laboratory tests** and **examinations** — results entered by your physician.
- **Imaging reports**, past surgeries, immunizations, allergies, chronic
  conditions.
- **My Physicians** and **My Clinics** — everyone you have consulted.

Your data is not tied to a single clinic: if you change physicians or cities,
your history stays with you. **You can export your data without restrictions on
any plan, including the free one.**

## You decide who sees what

When a physician or clinic requests access to your chart, you do not answer "yes"
or "no" for the whole record. Sections are opened **individually**:

- allergies;
- visits;
- encounters and the notes about them;
- imaging and reports;
- immunizations;
- past surgeries.

You can open allergies to a dentist and keep your surgical history closed. Access
is granted by you, not by an administrator at the front desk, and every access to
your chart is written to a log that cannot be altered or deleted.

## Your physician — in person or by video

You find a physician by specialty, review the profile, articles, other patients'
reviews and peer recommendations from colleagues, and book an available time slot
yourself.

- **Video visits** take place in the browser — nothing needs to be installed.
- **Chat with your physician** between visits: questions, files, and the entire
  correspondence is saved.
- **Messages are translated**: you write in your language, your physician reads
  in theirs. The language barrier is no longer a reason to look for "a physician
  who speaks my language."

## An in-depth review on your topic

If you have been given a diagnosis and want to understand it more deeply, the
platform will compile a detailed review on the topic with references to sources
and a "what this means in practice" block after each section. Not a three-paragraph
note, but material you can bring to your physician along with meaningful questions.

## Your data

- Personal and medical data is **encrypted** at rest.
- Every access to your chart is written to an **audit log**, which is retained
  for seven years and cannot be modified, even by an administrator.
- A physician gains access **only after your confirmation** and only to the
  sections you have opened.

The platform is built with regard for HIPAA requirements on the handling of
medical data.

## What it costs

- **Free — $0.** Indefinitely. 5 assistant consultations per month, 3 clinical
  summaries, your entire history, and unlimited data export.
- **Plus — $9 per month** ($90 per year). 30 consultations, 20 clinical
  summaries, 5 topic reviews, and a **10% discount on video visits** with a
  physician.
- **Pro — $19 per month** ($190 per year). Unlimited consultations and clinical
  summaries, 20 topic reviews, and a **20% discount on video visits**.

Storing your history, accessing it, and exporting your data are **free on all
plans**. The paid portion covers AI assistance and the discount on visits. Annual
payment equals ten monthly payments: two months free.

Separately, there is **medical exam preparation**: if you are a student or
resident, the platform's question bank is available as an add-on on top of any
plan, including the free one — **$7 per month** for 2000 questions or **$15 per
month** with no limits. A patient does not need it.

Current terms are always on the [pricing page](/pricing).

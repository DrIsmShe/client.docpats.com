# DocPats for Patients

Your medical history stays with you, not in someone else's records office: visits,
laboratory tests, images, immunizations, and past surgeries are gathered in one place. You
decide which physicians see what, and you can retrieve your data at any time.

**You can start without registering** — ask a question and get an answer before
creating an account.

---

## Ask Before the Visit

The medical assistant reviews your complaints, explains what your test results mean, and
suggests which specialist to see. It replies in your language — the one
you used to address it.

What it does **differently from a search engine**:

- **it does not make a diagnosis** — it provides a preliminary clinical assessment and always
  recommends an in-person visit;
- in the presence of red flags — chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of
  stroke — it immediately advises calling emergency services rather than continuing the conversation;
- it responds only to medical topics.

After a few messages, it will offer to compile a **discharge-style summary** — a brief digest
of the conversation: complaints, their duration, what has already been taken, what should be clarified. You
can bring it to your physician and avoid trying to recall in the office exactly when everything began.

This is not a substitute for a physician. It is preparation for the visit, so that the appointment begins with the essentials.

## Your Medical History — in One Place

- **Visits** — past and upcoming, with history.
- **Laboratory tests** and **examinations** — results entered by your physician.
- **Imaging reports**, past surgeries, immunizations, allergies,
  chronic conditions.
- **My Physicians** and **My Clinics** — everyone you have consulted.

Your data is not tied to a single clinic: change physicians or cities, and your history stays
with you. **You can export your data without restrictions on any plan, including
the free one.**

## You Decide Who Sees What

When a physician or clinic requests access to your chart, you do not answer "yes"
or "no" as a whole. Sections are opened **individually**:

- allergies;
- visits;
- encounters and their records;
- images and reports;
- immunizations;
- past surgeries.

You can open allergies to your dentist and keep your surgical history closed. Access is granted by
you, not by an administrator at the front desk, and every access to your chart is
written to a log that cannot be altered or deleted.

## Your Physician — in Person or by Video

You find a physician by specialty, review the profile, articles, other patients' reviews,
and colleagues' recommendations, and book an available time slot yourself.

- **Video visits** take place in the browser — nothing needs to be installed.
- **Chat with your physician** between visits: questions, files, and the entire correspondence is saved.
- **Messages are translated**: you write in your language, your physician reads in theirs.
  The language barrier is no longer a reason to look for "a physician who speaks your language."

## Your Data

- Personal and medical data are **encrypted** at rest.
- Every access to your chart is written to an **audit log**, which is retained
  for seven years and cannot be modified even by an administrator.
- A physician gains access **only after your confirmation** and only to those
  sections you have opened.

The platform is built with HIPAA requirements for handling medical
data in mind.

## What It Costs

**Everything related to your data is free and will remain free**: your entire
history, all documents, exports, booking with a physician, and correspondence with them. It only
makes sense to pay for AI assistance and for family member profiles.

Everything related to your health and your data is free and will remain
so: history, documents, exports, booking with a physician, correspondence with them,
and the visits themselves. **No plan limits your communication with your physician** —
how long a visit lasts is decided by you and your physician.

Only one thing is paid: how many times per month you can talk with
the AI assistant about your health.

- **Free — 0 $.** Unlimited duration. 2 assistant consultations per month.
  **The first 3 months after registration — 10.**
- **Plus — 9 $ per month** (90 $ per year). 15 consultations.

Annual payment equals ten monthly payments: two months free.

When the three months end, the account **reverts to free**, not
to paid. Charges never occur without your involvement: we do not request payment
details and do not enroll anyone in a paid plan — users choose it themselves.

Separately, there is **preparation for medical examinations**: if you are a student or
resident, the platform's question bank is unlocked as an add-on on top of any plan,
including the free one — **7 $ per month** adds timed practice,
**15 $ per month** adds a full examination simulation. A patient does not need it: the bank
is compiled from physician certification questions.

Current terms are always on the [pricing page](/pricing).

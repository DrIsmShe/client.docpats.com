# DocPats for Patients

Your medical history is kept by you, not in someone else's records room: visits,
laboratory tests, images, immunizations, and past surgeries are gathered in one
place. You decide which physicians see what, and you can retrieve your data at
any time.

**You can start without registering** — ask a question and get an answer before
creating an account.

---

## Ask Before the Visit

The medical assistant reviews your complaints, explains what your test results
mean, and suggests which specialist to see. It responds in your language — the
one you used to address it.

What it does **differently from a search engine**:

- **it does not make a diagnosis** — it provides a preliminary clinical
  assessment and always recommends an in-person visit;
- when warning signs are present — chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of
  stroke — it immediately advises calling emergency services rather than
  continuing the conversation;
- it responds only on medical topics.

After several messages, it will offer to compile a **discharge summary** — a
brief digest of the conversation: complaints, their duration, what has already
been taken, and what should be clarified. You can bring it to your physician
and avoid having to recall in the office exactly when everything started.

This is not a substitute for a physician. It is preparation for the visit, so
that the appointment begins with the essentials.

## Your Medical History — in One Place

- **Visits** — past and upcoming, with history.
- **Laboratory tests** and **examinations** — results entered by your physician.
- **Imaging reports**, past surgeries, immunizations, allergies, chronic
  conditions.
- **My Physicians** and **My Clinics** — everyone you have consulted.

Your data is not tied to a single clinic: if you change physicians or cities,
the history stays with you. **You can export your data without restrictions on
any plan, including the free one.**

## You Decide Who Sees What

When a physician or clinic requests access to your chart, you do not answer
"yes" or "no" for everything at once. Sections are opened **individually**:

- allergies;
- appointments;
- visits and their records;
- images and reports;
- immunizations;
- past surgeries.

You can open allergies to your dentist and keep your surgical history closed.
Access is granted by you, not by an administrator at the front desk, and every
access to your chart is written to a log that cannot be altered or deleted.

## Your Physician — In Person or by Video

You find a physician by specialty, review the profile, articles, other patients'
reviews, and colleagues' recommendations, and book an available time slot
yourself.

- **Video visits** take place in the browser — nothing needs to be installed.
- **Chat with your physician** between visits: questions, files, and the entire
  correspondence is saved.
- **Correspondence is translated**: you write in your language, your physician
  reads in theirs. The language barrier ceases to be a reason to search for a
  "physician who speaks your language."

## An In-Depth Review of Your Topic

If you have been given a diagnosis and want to understand it more deeply, the
platform will compile a detailed review of the topic with links to sources and a
"what this means in practice" section after each part. Not a three-paragraph
note, but material you can bring to your physician along with meaningful
questions.

## Your Data

- Personal and medical data are **encrypted** at rest.
- Every access to your chart is written to an **audit log**, which is retained
  for seven years and cannot be modified even by an administrator.
- A physician gains access **only after your confirmation** and only to the
  sections you have opened.

The platform is built with HIPAA requirements for handling medical data in mind.

## What It Costs

- **Free — $0.** Unlimited duration. 2 assistant consultations per month,
  1 discharge summary, your entire history, and data export without restrictions.
- **Plus — $9 per month** ($90 per year). 10 consultations, 10 discharge
  summaries, 2 topic reviews, and a **10% discount on video visits** with a
  physician.
- **Pro — $19 per month** ($190 per year). 25 consultations, 25 discharge
  summaries, 8 reviews, and a **20% discount on video visits**.

Storing your history, accessing it, and exporting your data are **free on all
plans**. The paid portion covers AI assistance and the visit discount. Annual
payment equals ten monthly payments: two months free.

Separately, there is **medical exam preparation**: if you are a student or
resident, the platform's question bank is available as an add-on on top of any
plan, including the free one — **$7 per month** for 2000 questions or
**$15 per month** with no restrictions. Patients do not need it.

Current terms are always on the [pricing page](/pricing).

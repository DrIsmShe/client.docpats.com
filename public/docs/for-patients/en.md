# DocPats for Patients

Your medical history is kept by you, not in someone else's records room: visits,
laboratory tests, images, immunizations, and past surgeries are all gathered in
one place. You decide which physicians see what, and you can retrieve your data
at any time.

**You can start without registering** — ask a question and get an answer before
creating an account.

---

## Ask Before the Visit

The medical assistant reviews your symptoms, explains what your test results
mean, and suggests which specialist to see. It answers in your language — the
one you used to contact it.

What it does **differently from a search engine**:

- **it does not make a diagnosis** — it provides a preliminary clinical
  assessment and always recommends an in-person visit;
- in the presence of red flags — chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of
  stroke — it immediately advises calling emergency services rather than
  continuing the conversation;
- it responds only to medical topics.

After a few messages it will offer to compile a **clinical summary** — a brief
digest of the conversation: symptoms, their duration, what you have already
taken, what should be clarified. You can bring it to your physician and avoid
trying to recall in the office exactly when everything began.

This is not a substitute for a physician. It is preparation for the visit, so
that the appointment starts with what matters.

## Your Medical History — in One Place

- **Visits** — past and upcoming, with their history.
- **Laboratory tests** and **examinations** — results entered by your physician.
- **Imaging reports**, past surgeries, immunizations, allergies, chronic
  conditions.
- **My Physicians** and **My Clinics** — everyone you have consulted.

Your data is not tied to a single clinic: if you change physicians or cities,
your history stays with you. **You can export your data without restrictions on
any plan, including the free one.**

## You Decide Who Sees What

When a physician or clinic requests access to your chart, you do not answer
"yes" or "no" for everything at once. Sections are opened **individually**:

- allergies;
- visits;
- encounters and their records;
- images and reports;
- immunizations;
- past surgeries.

You can open allergies to a dentist while keeping your surgical history closed.
Access is granted by you, not by an administrator at the front desk, and every
access to your chart is written to a log that cannot be altered or deleted.

## Your Physician — In Person or by Video

You find a physician by specialty, review their profile, articles, reviews from
other patients, and recommendations from fellow physicians, and book an
available time slot yourself.

- **Video visits** take place in the browser — nothing needs to be installed.
- **Chat with your physician** between visits: questions, files, and the entire
  correspondence is saved.
- **Messages are translated**: you write in your language, your physician reads
  in theirs. The language barrier is no longer a reason to look for "a physician
  who speaks my language."

## An In-Depth Review on Your Topic

If you have received a diagnosis and want to understand it more deeply, the
platform will compile a detailed review on the topic with references to sources
and a "what this means in practice" section after each part. Not a three-
paragraph note, but material you can take to your physician along with
meaningful questions.

## Your Data

- Personal and medical data are **encrypted** at rest.
- Every access to your chart is written to an **audit log** that is retained for
  seven years and cannot be modified, even by an administrator.
- A physician gains access **only after your confirmation** and only to the
  sections you have opened.

The platform is built with HIPAA requirements for handling medical data in mind.

## What It Costs

- **Free — $0.** No expiration. 5 assistant consultations per month, 3 clinical
  summaries, your complete history, and unlimited data export.
- **Plus — $9 per month** ($90 per year). 30 consultations, 20 clinical
  summaries, 5 topic reviews, and a **10% discount on video visits** with a
  physician.
- **Pro — $19 per month** ($190 per year). Unlimited consultations and clinical
  summaries, 20 reviews, and a **20% discount on video visits**.

Storing your history, accessing it, and exporting your data are **free on all
plans**. The paid portion covers AI assistance and the visit discount.
